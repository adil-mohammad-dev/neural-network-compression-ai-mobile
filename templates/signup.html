Neural Network Compression Techniques For Deploying AI Models On Mobile Devices In Rural India

Project Overview

This system focuses on optimizing and compressing deep learning models to make AI more accessible and efficient on mobile devices in rural India. Using cutting-edge techniques such as pruning, quantization, and knowledge distillation, our platform ensures faster inference, lower memory usage, and improved accuracy — all while supporting offline environments.
